6.db.orders.find({status:{$in:["PENDING", "FAILED"]}})

7. Retrieve how many orders exists for each status. 

Idea: 
- Groups orders by status

Answer:
db.orders.aggregate([{$group:{_id: "$status", count:{$sum:1}}}])
-------------------------------------------------------
8. Retrieve highest order amount per city. 

Idea:
-Use $max for highest value

Answer:
db.orders.aggregate([{$group:{_id:"$city, maxAmount:{$max:"$amount"}}}])
--------------------------------------------------------------------
9. Calculate average order in each city
Idea:
-Use $avg to calculate average

Answer:
db.orders.aggregate([{$group:{_id:"$city", avgAmount:{$avg:"$amount"}}}])
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
10. Sort the cities in DESC by total sales
Idea:
-group sales by city
- sort the output in DESC

Answer:
db.orders.aggregate([{$group:{_id:"$city", totalSales:{$sum: "$amount"}}}, {$sort:{totalSales:-1}}])

 -------------------------------------------------
11. Count Customers whose name begin with 'A'
Answer:
db.orders.aggregate([{$match:{customer:{$regex:"^A"}}}]),
{$count:"ordersbyAcustomers"}])
-----------------------------------------------------
12. Count orders from cities starting with "K"
Answer:
db.orders.aggregate([{$match:{city:{$regex:"^K"}}}, {$count:"orderfromKcities"}])
------------------------------------------------------
13. Calculate total sales of PAID orders for customers names ends with "i"
Answer:
db.orders.aggregate([{$match:{customer:{$regex:"i$"}, status: "PAID"}}, {$group:{_id:"$customer", totalSales:{$sum:"$amount"}}}])

Idea:
-$match : i$ (ending with i), status: PAID
-group by customers
-------------------------------------------------------
14. Count total quantity sold per product (type/sku)

db.orders.aggregate([{$unwind:"$items"},{$group:{_id:"$items.type", totalQty:{$sum:"$items.qty"}}}])

Idea:
- $unwind : group by array fields, aggregate per array
(usually it is mandatory to use $unwind as its acts as flattening step so that can process the element separately)
------------------------------------------------------------
15. Retrieve orders that contain SKU/TYPE "A1" with a quantity of 2 or more. 

- recall documents

db.orders.find({}, {_id:0, orderId: 1, customer: 1, items:1})

db.orders.find({"items.sku":"A1"})
db.orders.find({"items.qty":{$gte: 2}})

db.orders.find({items:{$elemMatch:{sku:"A1", qty: {$gte: 2}}}})

Idea:
-$elemMatch 
- gte and gt : gte = greater than and equals to, gt = greater than
